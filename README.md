### ✦ About Me

I am a Full-Stack Engineer and Creative Technologist who bridges the gap between high-end digital art direction and resilient software engineering. As the founder of **Clutch Code**, I lead the architecture of high-performance web systems, hardware-accelerated animations, and cloud-native multi-tenant applications.

- 🔭 **Current Focus**: GPU-accelerated motion choreography, interactive 3D WebGL architectures, and enterprise SaaS.
- ⚡ **Core Craft**: Hardware-accelerated Canvas & GSAP scrubbing, Next.js / TypeScript systems, and ASP.NET Core cloud services.
- 📍 **Headquarters**: Kerala, India.
